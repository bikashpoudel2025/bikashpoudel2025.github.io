Research & Engineering Projects

These projects reflect the problems I have explored through engineering coursework and research. Reported gains from simulation and what-if analyses describe modeled scenarios, not necessarily changes implemented at a plant or hospital.

AnyLogic · Agent-based and discrete-event simulation

Influenza Spread and Hospital Surge Response

Developed a hybrid agent-based and discrete-event model to explore how influenza transmission affects hospital operations. Queue and utilization analysis pointed to ICU nursing capacity as a key constraint. In the modeled staffing scenarios, patient time in the system fell by approximately 55%.

Excel · NPV and sensitivity analysis

Strategic Investment Decisions Under Uncertainty

Compared a large upfront investment with a staged investment strategy using net present value. Explored how changes in demand and costs affected the financial case for each option.

Python · Machine learning

Student Depression Prediction

Worked with 27,898 student records and compared Random Forest, Decision Tree, KNN, and Logistic Regression models. The reported results reached 84.1% accuracy and 83.0% recall. The work included preprocessing, exploratory analysis, five-fold cross-validation, and feature importance.

SAS · Applied regression

Understanding Life Expectancy Through Regression

Analyzed data from 179 countries to investigate factors associated with life expectancy. Built multiple linear regression models with a reported R² of 0.957 and checked model assumptions using VIF, interaction terms, residual diagnostics, Breusch–Pagan testing, normality, and influence analysis.

Siemens Plant Simulation · Throughput analysis

Dual Manufacturing Line Optimization

Examined production constraints in a proposed dual-line system and tested feasible changes. In the simulation, throughput increased from 3.9 to 5 jobs per hour, an improvement of about 28%.

Systems engineering · Functional flow diagrams

JLTV Systems Engineering Requirements

Applied systems engineering principles to define and allocate requirements for a Joint Light Tactical Vehicle system at the system, subsystem, and component levels, using functional flow block diagrams.

Value stream mapping · Time studies

Lean Process Improvement in Coffee Shops

Studied processes at Starbucks, Biggby, and Honcho coffee shops in Oakland County. Used time studies, value stream mapping, root-cause analysis, and visual controls to identify unnecessary steps and improve inventory and workflow practices.

Arena · Discrete-event simulation

Vehicle Assembly Plant Simulation

Modeled vehicle assembly operations to identify bottlenecks and determine a suitable number of pallets. The simulated line increased from 62 to 68 jobs per hour.

DMAIC · Statistical quality control

Six Sigma in Noodle Manufacturing

For my undergraduate engineering project, analyzed variation in noodle weights and applied the DMAIC framework to identify ways to keep production within specification limits.
